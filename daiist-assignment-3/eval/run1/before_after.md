**Question (q36):** How far in advance must a wheelchair user notify the airline to get assistance at the airport?

## After

To ensure assistance at the airport, a passenger with reduced mobility should notify the air carrier, their agent, or the tour operator at least **48 hours before the published departure time** of the flight [air-prm-1107-2006, Article 7(1)]. This notification also covers a return flight if it is booked with the same carrier [air-prm-1107-2006, Article 7(1)].

If you are traveling with an electric wheelchair, you must provide this 48-hour advance warning to the airline [air-prm-1107-2006, Annex I; air-prm-1107-2006, Annex II].

If notification is not made at least 48 hours in advance, the airport managing body is still required to make "all reasonable efforts" to provide the necessary assistance so that the passenger can take their flight [air-prm-1107-2006, Article 7(3)].

Additionally, to receive the assistance, the passenger must arrive at the designated point at the airport at the time stipulated by the carrier, or if no time is specified, at least one or two hours before the published departure time, depending on the specific requirements [air-prm-1107-2006, Article 7(4)].
